Week 12: The week the waiver wire cried and special teams stole someone's lunch money

If fantasy football were a soap opera, Week 12 was the episode where the janitor (kickers/DSTs) walked in with a machine gun and emptied the plot. Explosions from a single RB, bench‑management malpractice, and a 0.26-point nailbiter made it feel less like a sport and more like a reality show with spreadsheets.

Week 12 in one sentence: One running back (Jahmyr Gibbs) screamed so loud the rest of the league had to change their pickup lines — and several managers will be paying for benching mistakes in therapy.
