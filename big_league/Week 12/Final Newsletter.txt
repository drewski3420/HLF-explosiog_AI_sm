Top matchups (recaps, with headlines)

Jake Butt Stuff (116.26) over Team Santangelo (78.96) — Ravens D/ST flexes while Santangelo naps 🦅
Jake Butt Stuff rolled to a comfortable 116.26–78.96 win despite some familiar heartbreak: CeeDee Lamb underperformed and J.B.S. even had J.J. McCarthy collecting bench cobwebs. Team Santangelo’s QB room was gutted by BYEs/inactives (Herbert & Ekeler BYE; Chase/LaPorta inactive), and the bench tragedy continues — Christian Kirk scored 16.4 but sat like a polite Thanksgiving guest.  
Time‑travel quip — Team Santangelo: To Wednesday night, to ask, “Did I leave Kirk on the bench?”  
Decisive blunder: Bench Kirk + multiple inactives = easy loss.

Big Trouble in Little Cameron (139.86) over Dickhole Smashers (31.6) — football massacre at the buffet 🍽️
BTiLC put up 139.86 (Dak and DJ Moore doing the heavy lifting) while the Smashers managed a season-low 31.6 after a litany of inactives. The Smashers had Emanuel Wilson sitting on a 26.5-day party bench and Aaron Rodgers/Isiah Pacheco inactive — the kind of roster management that will earn you looks you won’t enjoy at Thanksgiving.  
Time‑travel quip — Dickhole Smashers: Back to the waiver wire with a pilgrim hat and zero grace.  
Decisive blunder: Leaving Emanuel Wilson on the pine was criminally inefficient.

Trying to not finish last (94.22) over Kyler? I Barely Know Her (92.28) — a two‑point dogfight decided by George Pickens 🐾
Trying to not finish last squeaked by 94.22–92.28 in a matchup where Harrison Butker’s leg and a tidy floor saved the bacon. George Pickens was the matchup’s showstopper for KIBKH (a huge outlier versus projection), but Bijan Robinson and Lions D/ST disappointed, and Kyler Murray was inactive on the bench (yes, actually inactive).  
Time‑travel quip — KIBKH: To the draft board, with a sticky note that reads “Kyler: don’t trust your luck.”  
Decisive play: Pickens’ boom day kept it close, but Murray inactive and underperforming RBs did KIBKH in.

The Philly Special (120.76) over Kraft Montgomery Cheese (108.52) — Hurts and A‑Rod carry the turkey platter 🏈
Philly’s Jalen Hurts + Amon‑Ra St. Brown combo nudged The Philly Special to 120.76, while Kraft’s WRs had good games but several starters underperformed against projections. Chimere Dike ironically collected 21.4 points on Philly’s bench — a recurring theme: someone’s sweets are always left on the counter.  
Time‑travel quip — Kraft Montgomery Cheese: To the concession stand, hoping they’ll exchange Dalton Schultz for a casserole.

Hangin' With Mr. Cooper (116.16) over 5WRsAllowedOnRostersNextYear (110.42) — D/STs and special teams steal Thanksgiving sides 🧤
Hangin’ won a close one largely on Kenneth Gainwell and elite D/ST output; 5WRs eked out non‑WR value (Rams/Packers D/STs and K Andy Borregales helped) but couldn’t quite close the gap. In classic league fashion, multiple offensive starters for 5WRs were quiet while kickers/defenses turned into fantasy linebackers.  
Time‑travel quip — 5WRsAllowedOnRostersNextYear: Back to 2019, when people respected kickers.  
Decisive detail: D/ST/kicker contributions were the margin-of-victory story.

THE Derbaum (115.4) over If Ur Not First You're Last (68.98) — balance beats volatility 🧩
THE Derbaum’s mix-and-match scoring (including a Parker Washington surprise) toppled If Ur, who was gutted by RB non‑production and poor projection follow‑through. If Ur’s usually lofty ceiling cratered and left them looking like they’d ordered a five‑star menu and got a side salad.  
Time‑travel quip — THE Derbaum: To the trophy case, polishing already (premature, but let him dream).  
Decisive blunder: If Ur’s RBs underperforming (Henry/Swift issues) killed any comeback.

Forte Inch Johnson (116.42) over Team Jon (110.92) — Jahmyr’s fireworks, and a bench Goff left sulking 🎆
Forte’s Jahmyr Gibbs and Jaxon Smith‑Njigba exploded and almost carried the day alone; Team Jon’s Jahmyr was active too but Tyreek Hill BYE and Josh Allen volatility left them short. Team Jon left Jared Goff’s 17.16 on the bench, which stings when a game ends that close.  
Time‑travel quip — Team Jon: To the waiver knoll, pleading for a miracle claim.

Xavier Marks the Spot (122.04) over SnydeRemarks (91.72) — A.J. Brown and Etienne do the heavy lifting; SnydeRemarks misses Burrow 🏴‍☠️
Xavier rode A.J. Brown and Travis Etienne Jr. to 122.04, while SnydeRemarks suffered from an inactive Joe Burrow and a general down week among WRs/TEs. Hunter Henry and Dylan Sampson were lonely on Xavier’s bench with a combined 40+, which is the seventh circle of roster remorse.  
Time‑travel quip — SnydeRemarks: To the IR list, to beg for return dates like a dog begging for scraps.
